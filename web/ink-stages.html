How the ink went from a ring to a living notch

Seven stages, all moving at once.

Still ink ring

Solid blob

The ring fills in. A turning cluster of liquid blobs with droplets. Sharp edges, slow and oily. Only reacts to a real voice; wakes on “orange”.

Lumpy head, first gloss

Uneven lobes from the first sound and limbs that wander, so it stops looking round. First oil highlight, too bright.

Subtle gloss

Highlight rebuilt from your photos: dim warm glow, thin line inside the edge. Smoother motion, spread halved.

Notch Alive
